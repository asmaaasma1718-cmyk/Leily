Payment Methods

Choose the payment methods your store accepts. These settings will apply to your store.

💵 Cash on delivery
The customer pays when receiving the order.
💳 Full payment in advance
The customer transfers the full order amount after the seller accepts the order.
💰 Half payment in advance
The customer pays half of the order amount before shipping and the remaining amount when receiving the order.
Important:
CCP and RIP information will not be displayed publicly on the seller's store page. They will only be shown to the customer after the seller accepts an order that requires advance payment.
